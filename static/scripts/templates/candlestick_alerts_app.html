HAMMER: A hammer is a single candle bullish reversal candlestick pattern. It appears at the end of a decline. The candle formation looks like a hammer, as it has a long lower wick and a short body at the top of the candlestick with little or no upper wick.

SHOOTING STAR: A shooting star is a single candle bearish reversal candlestick pattern. It appears at the end of a rise. The candle formation has a long upper wick and a short body at the end of the candlestick with little or no upper wick.

SPINNING TOP: A Spinning Top pattern involves a single candle indicating uncertainty in the market. The candlestick itself is defined by a short body surrounded by long wicks on either side. The Spinning Top can be either bullish or bearish at the candle close. If it has volumes accompanying it, it is even more powerful as a reversal setup.

DOJI: A doji is a candle formation where the security has an open and close that are virtually equal. Doji highlights that the ongoing trend is taking a breather and the trends may stall for the moment.

ENGULFING: Engulfing represents either bullish or bearish and is a two-candle pattern formation.
Bullish Engulfing is spotted at the end of the decline where the real body of the second candle (green) is covering the entire body of the first candle (red).
Bearish Engulfing is spotted at the end of the rise where the real body of the second candle (red) is covering the entire body of the first candle (green).

LONGLINE: Longline represents a large body candle that displays strong buying or selling activity has taken place in that particular time frame. It suggests that there is a possibility of more trended action to emerge.

LONG EXHAUSTION : When consistently rising candles are found back to back for a certain number, then a long exhaustion is signaled. One can exit longs on this signal after a red candle is formed.

SHORT EXHAUSTION : When consistently falling candles are found back to back for a certain number, then a short exhaustion is signaled. One can exit shorts on this signal after a green candle is formed.
