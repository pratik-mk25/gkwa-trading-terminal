    Day Trader Bullets - Guide

    * Stock - Trading symbol of the stock.

    * Day Change - Price change from previous trading day close to LTP.

    * Trigger Time - Time when trade signal was generated.

    * Trigger Price - Market price at which trade was triggered.

    * Move Since Trigger - Movement in points from trigger price to LTP.

    * I Expect - Calculated target price expansion.

    * My Risk - Calculated protective risk stoploss.

    Active Stocks - Strategy Guide

    * WELL SET BULL - Stocks showing established continued bullish momentum.

    * WELL SET BEAR - Stocks showing established continued bearish momentum.

    * TAKING GUARD BULL - Stocks turning bullish on the daily timeframe.

    * TAKING GUARD BEAR - Stocks turning bearish on the daily timeframe.

    Note: Active stocks refresh at 5-minute intervals. Grey cards indicate stalled momentum whenever price moves against the trend.
